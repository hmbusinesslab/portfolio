Nice to meet you!

I was born and raised in Tunisia, a country whose transformation deeply shaped the way I see the world. Growing up around the Tunisian Revolution sparked an early curiosity in me about change, opportunity, and the way new ideas can reshape economies and societies. That curiosity gradually led me from studying English Language and British Civilisation to Business Design, and later to doctoral studies focused on the integration of artificial intelligence into new business models. Today, at 26, I take on projects that allow me to explore the intersection of business, technology, and innovation—turning complex ideas into clearer systems, stronger models, and opportunities built for what comes next.

Doctoral research

Business design and AI integration in new business frameworks.

"Frameworks age. The organisations that outgrow theirs the fastest are the ones that never questioned the design in the first place."

The doctoral research examines how emerging AI capability changes the way business frameworks should be designed — not as a layer added on top of an existing model, but as a factor that reshapes the model itself.

It looks at where traditional frameworks break down under AI integration, and proposes a more adaptive approach to structuring decision-making, data flow, and operating models around it.
